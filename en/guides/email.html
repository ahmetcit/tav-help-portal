Email app: eM Client

Because Microsoft support ends at year-end, MS Outlook will no longer be used. Instead, the whole company is moving to eM Client — an Outlook-like app that works with all email formats and operating systems.

Download ↗ emclient.com ↗

Basic setup

  1. Download the version for your OS from emclient.com (Windows / macOS).
  2. Run the installer and finish setup.
  3. On first launch use "Add Account" and enter your @tav.engineering address and password (you can also sign in with your Google Workspace account).
  4. eM Client configures the incoming/outgoing servers automatically.

Interface

eM Client brings Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Tasks and Chat into one window — folders on the left, toolbar on top, message list in the middle. It feels very much like Outlook.

AI tools

eM Client offers AI-assisted writing, reply suggestions and summarization via its built-in AI assistant.

Corporate email signature

Your signature is uploaded to your account automatically:

  • Sent emails use the HTML (visual) signature.
  • Replies use a short plain-text signature.
