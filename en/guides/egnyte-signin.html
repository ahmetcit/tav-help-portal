Egnyte sign-in guide

This short guide shows you how to sign in to tavcloud.egnyte.com with your Google Workspace (work) account and add extra security with Google Authenticator.

Before you begin, have these ready

You only need three things. Check them off before you start.

  • Your work email address (the one that ends with @tav.engineering).
  • Your work password — the same one you use for Google / Gmail at work.
  • A smartphone (iPhone or Android) with access to the App Store or Google Play.

The steps, one at a time

Take it slowly. Tick each box as you finish it — your progress is saved on this page.

0 of 6 complete

1

Open Egnyte and choose “Sign in with Google”

Open your web browser and go to tavcloud.egnyte.com.

Open Egnyte ↗

On the sign-in page, look for the button that says “Sign in with Google” and click it.

2

Sign in with your Google Workspace account

A Google window will open. Pick your work account from the list (or type your @tav.engineering email and password if asked).

Which one is my work account?

It’s your @tav.engineering address — not a personal @gmail.com address.

3

Meet “two-step verification” (MFA)

To keep your files safe, Egnyte asks for two things before letting you in:

  • Something you know — your password.
  • Something you have — your phone, showing a short code.

This second step is called multi-factor authentication (MFA) or two-step verification. It just means: even if someone learns your password, they still can’t get in without your phone.

4

Download the Google Authenticator app

Google Authenticator is a free app that shows you the security codes. Install it from your phone’s app store:

App Store Google Play

Search for “Google Authenticator” and look for the app made by Google LLC. It’s free and takes a minute to install.

5

Set it up by scanning a QR code (one time only)

The first time Egnyte asks for the extra code, it will show you a QR code on your computer screen.

  1. Open the Google Authenticator app on your phone.
  2. Tap the + button and choose “Scan a QR code”.
  3. Point your phone’s camera at the QR code on your screen.

That’s it — the app now knows about your account and will show a 6-digit code. You only do this once.

6

Enter the 6-digit code to finish

Open Google Authenticator and read the code next to your account name — it looks like 123 456 (that one is just an example).

Type the code into Egnyte and press Verify or Continue. You’re in! 🎉

The code changes every 30 seconds

If a code is about to expire (the little circle is nearly empty), wait a moment and use the fresh one.

Help & quick tips

A few things people often ask. Tap a question to open it.

My 6-digit code says it’s wrong

The most common fix: make sure your phone’s clock is set to automatic time (Settings → Date & Time). If the clock is even a little off, the codes won’t match. Also check you’re reading the code for the right account.

I got a new phone. How do I move Authenticator?

On your new phone, install Google Authenticator, then sign in to your Google account inside the app — your codes can be restored. Or ask your IT team to re-issue your QR code so you can scan it again.

What if I lose my phone or it runs out of battery?

Tell your IT team right away — they can temporarily help you get in and set things up again. This is exactly why two-step verification protects your account: no phone, no one else gets in either.

Do I need to enter a code every single time?

Often you can tick “Remember this device” when signing in, so you won’t be asked again on that same computer or phone for a while. The extra code is mostly asked on new devices or when extra security is needed.

Is Google Authenticator safe? Where are my codes stored?

Yes. The app stores your codes on your phone only, and nothing is sent to Google. Codes are generated on the spot and change every 30 seconds, so they can’t be guessed or reused.

Never share your code

No real company or IT person will ever ask you to read out your 6-digit code. If someone does, it’s a scam — hang up or ignore the message.
