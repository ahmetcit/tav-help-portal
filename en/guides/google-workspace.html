My account (myaccount.google.com)

All your Google account settings live at myaccount.google.com.

Open my account ↗

  • Personal info: name, photo, contact details.
  • Security: password, two-step verification, devices.
  • Data & privacy: activity and storage settings.

Password management

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com.
  2. Open Security in the left menu.
  3. Under "Signing in to Google → Password", change your password.
  4. Choose a strong password you don't use anywhere else.

Forgot it?

If you forgot your password, use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen and follow the recovery steps.

Google Meet for meetings

Quick guide

  1. Go to meet.google.com (or open Meet from Gmail's left menu).
  2. Use "New meeting" to start instantly, schedule for later, or create an invite link.
  3. Copy the link and share it with participants.
  4. During the meeting, control microphone, camera and screen sharing from the bottom bar.

Tip

Test your camera and microphone before joining; you can also blur your background.
