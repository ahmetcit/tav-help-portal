Passwords & Accounts

All password management is handled through Google Workspace.

  1. Google Authenticator — MFA setup
  • All passwords are managed through Google Workspace.
  • Access to email and TAVcloud (Egnyte) accounts is tied to your Google account.
  • The Google Authenticator app is used for two-step verification (MFA).

Google Authenticator — MFA setup

Computer app setup

  1. Sign in to your Google account in your browser.
  2. Go to Security → 2-Step Verification.
  3. Add an Authenticator app; a QR code will appear.
  4. If you use an authenticator extension/pairing tool on the computer, scan the QR code with it.
  5. Enter the 6-digit code to complete setup.

Mobile app setup

  1. Install Google Authenticator on your phone:
    App Store Google Play
  2. Open the app, tap + and choose "Scan a QR code".
  3. Point your phone camera at the QR code on your computer screen.
  4. The app now produces a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.

Never share your code

No real company or IT person will ever ask you to read out your 6-digit code. If someone does, it's a scam.
